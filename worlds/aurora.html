The Foundation

What is the Law of Attraction?

Like attracts like. The energy you radiate — in thoughts, feelings and attention — returns to you in similar form.

Imagine you're a radio transmitter. The frequency you broadcast on — what you feel, think and believe — determines which station you tune into. A station full of fear and lack receives news about fear and lack. A station full of trust, gratitude and love receives the opposite.

Manifestation isn't a wish list to the cosmos. It's the conscious tuning of your inner world so your outer world can follow. You don't attract what you want — you attract what you bent.

The three pillars

  • Asking — Creating clarity about what you really want (not what you think you should want).
  • Believing — Feeling, behaving and thinking as if it's already on its way.
  • Receiving — Releasing the 'how' and acting from inspiration, not from lack.

An important nuance

Manifestation is no replacement for medical help, professional therapy or practical action. See it as an inner discipline — a way to change your relationship with yourself and your goals — alongside, not instead of, everything that already works in your life.

Step by Step

How to manifest

A complete seven-step guide. Walk through them in order, or pick the step that feels like what you need now. It doesn't have to be ethereal, complicated or perfect. Mostly it just needs to be done.

The simple formula

Manifesting isn't a trick where you wish once and wait. It's a daily way of thinking, feeling and choosing. You place attention on what you want, practise the feeling as if it's already safe to receive, and take small actions that fit that version of you.

  • Choose clearly — one longing at a time, as concrete as possible.
  • Feel consciously — summon the feeling that belongs to your longing.
  • Act small — take one action today that fits.
  • Repeat — better five minutes a day than one intense session.

If you remember nothing else: think it, feel it, do something that fits.

Be painfully clear

Vague longing gives vague results. Write down: what do you want exactly, why do you want it, and how does it feel when you have it? Describe it in present tense, as if it's already here. Not: "I don't want money worries any more." But: "I live in financial calm and have room to be generous."

Example: not "I want to be happy", but "I wake with peace in my body, I have work that gives me energy, and I feel space in my schedule."

Do now: write one sentence beginning with: "I choose to build a life where..." Make it concrete enough to recognise when it happens.

Feel it — now

Feeling is the magnet, not the thought. Ask yourself: how would I feel if this were true? Proud? Free? Safe? Playful? Summon that feeling now, even if the situation isn't here yet. This is the core of everything.

Example: want more money? The deeper feeling may be safety, space, or freedom of choice. Practise safety today by calmly looking at your finances for 10 minutes, not by panic-checking everything.

Do now: pick one feeling and set a timer for 60 seconds. Breathe as if that feeling already lives in your body.

Visualise daily

Close your eyes, 5 to 10 minutes. See the scene where your longing is fulfilled. What do you smell? Who is there with you? What are you wearing? What do you say to yourself? The more sensory detail, the stronger the signal.

Example: want a new job? Don't just see the contract. See yourself walking in on your first lovely day, hanging up your coat, opening your laptop and thinking: "this fits."

Do now: make your visualisation small enough to believe. One scene is enough. Repeat the same scene for seven days.

Write as if it happened

Grab a notebook. Write one page from your 'already manifested' self. "Today I woke up in my new house…" or "I still can't believe the job interview went so well." Your brain doesn't strongly distinguish between vividly imagined and actually experienced.

Example: "Today I got the message I was hoping for. It didn't feel like coincidence, it felt as if my life was finally working with me. I was calm, not surprised. As if a part of me already knew."

Do now: write 10 lines with this opener: "The most beautiful thing about how this turned out is..." Stop before you start doubting.

Become the version of you who already has it

Which habits, clothes, language, decisions belong to the you already living this longing? Start there today — small. If she rises early, you do too. If she sets boundaries, you practise too. Identity first, circumstances follow.

Example: someone treated lovingly doesn't run for hours after a half reply. Someone living abundance doesn't talk to themselves all day as if everything is lacking.

Do now: ask yourself: "What would the version of me who already has this no longer do today?" Pick one thing and let it go today.

Act on inspirations

The universe rarely 'delivers' via a postman. It delivers via ideas, meetings, curiosity, opportunities that suddenly appear. Say yes to inspirations pointing in the right direction — even if you don't see how they connect.

Example: you suddenly think of someone you could email, a course to look at, a place to go, a room to tidy. It doesn't have to be big. Movement opens doors.

Do now: note three small inspirations you had this week. Pick the lightest and do it within 24 hours.

Let go — but stay present

The paradox: you hold the vision and you leave the how, when and what-form to something larger. Clinging to control is distrust dressed up as diligence. Letting go doesn't mean stopping — it means trusting it's already on the way.

Example: you may long for a relationship, but you don't have to decide it must be that one person, this week, through that one message. Hold on to love. Let the route breathe.

Do now: write: "I release the how. I stay available for the best outcome." Read it aloud, then go do something ordinary in your life.

A short daily ritual

Don't have time for seven steps every day? Do this minimum:

  • Morning — 2 minutes visualising + one affirmation out loud
  • During the day — One small action as if you already are it
  • Evening — Write down three things you're grateful for

Five minutes a day. Consistency beats intensity.

The 7-day do-assignment

Don't want to stay stuck in reading? Do this for one week. Simple, concrete, doable.

  • Day 1 — Pick one longing and write it in one clear sentence.
  • Day 2 — Write why you want this and what feeling lies underneath.
  • Day 3 — Visualise one scene for 3 minutes.
  • Day 4 — Do one action that fits the version of you already having this.
  • Day 5 — Rewrite one limiting belief into a helping belief.
  • Day 6 — Practise receiving: accept a compliment, help, or chance without making it smaller.
  • Day 7 — Write three pieces of evidence your life is already moving.

When you get stuck

Stop looking for proof that it isn\'t working. That is also manifesting, but towards fear. Come back to your breath, to one question: what would I do today if I knew it will be okay?

Do that. Small enough to do today. Real enough to turn your energy.

Common blockages

"I've been trying for months, nothing's happening"

You\'re probably trying from lack. The longing feels like "I don\'t have this yet" instead of "this is coming to me." The difference is subtle but essential. Stop manifesting for a week and focus only on gratitude for what\'s already here.

"I don't really believe in it anymore"

Good that you acknowledge that. Don\'t force belief — that creates division. Start smaller: manifest something unimportant (a free coffee, an unexpected message, a green wave in traffic). Evidence to yourself that it works at a small scale. Trust grows, it\'s rarely jumped to.

"I don't know what I want"

Then that is your work. Not manifesting — discovering. Write three minutes every morning about: what feels like 'yes' in my life? What feels like 'no'? Longing often gets drowned out by what you think you should want. Silence restores the signal.

"I get frustrated it's taking so long"

Frustration is a broadcast on the frequency 'it isn\'t here.' So you receive 'it isn\'t here' back. This isn\'t to punish yourself — it\'s to recognise that impatience itself is a blockage. A good question: what would I do today if I knew for sure it will arrive in two weeks?

Writing prompts when you have no idea what to manifest

  • If I knew I couldn't fail, this year I would…
  • The version of me I already am deep down would…
  • What would happen if I let myself want what I really want?
  • What would a calm, fulfilled version of me do differently today?
  • Which three words do I want my life to radiate in a year?
  • What do I actually already have that I haven't yet acknowledged as 'manifested'?

The very last advice

When everything stalls: live your life. The energy you attract doesn't come from how hard you manifest. It comes from how full, present and alive you are while you wait. A full life is a magnet. A fixated life is a waiting room.

Why the moon?

For centuries people have attuned their rhythms to the moon — for sowing crops, for rituals, for reflection. Science recognises the effect on tides. Esoteric traditions see the moon as a mirror of the inner: as she waxes and wanes, so do we.

Working with the moon phases gives your manifestation a natural rhythm. Not everything is always a time to create — sometimes it's time to let go, sometimes to wait. The moon reminds you of that.

How to use the oracle well

  • Ask an open question — not "will I get X?" but "what should I know about X?"
  • Don't draw five times in one day. One reading per question.
  • Read the card literally and symbolically. What stood out?
  • Write down what you felt when you saw the message — that's where the answer is.
