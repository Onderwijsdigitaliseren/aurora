On longing, ego, respect and learning

Longing isn't the ego you must overcome. It's often your soul telling you where life wants to go.

The misunderstanding about the ego

There exists an idea — popular in some spiritual circles — that everything you want for yourself is "ego", and that you are only truly spiritual once you have no longings left. Wanting money? Ego. Wanting recognition? Ego. And so every longing suddenly feels like a shortcoming.

But that's a narrowing. In almost no wisdom tradition are you asked to erase yourself. What they describe is subtler: not clinging, not hanging your whole identity on what you own. That\'s something very different from being forbidden to want anything.

The problem is never the longing itself. It\'s only the fear beneath it — and you don\'t solve that by denying the longing.

Money is simply energy

Money is exchanged trust, a way that flow becomes visible. Asking for it is as legitimate as asking for health or love. Having enough gives calm, room to give, and freedom to care for others without exhausting yourself.

Respect and recognition may be there

Wanting to be seen and appreciated isn\'t vanity — it\'s a deeply human need. It only becomes shaky when you place your self-worth entirely in someone else\'s hands. The healthy version: I may long for respect and I know my worth doesn\'t disappear if someone doesn\'t give it to me for a moment.

Loving respect for yourself

Respecting yourself also means taking your boundaries seriously. Constantly pleasing others and erasing yourself feels like love, but exhausts you and teaches people that your needs don\'t count. Setting a boundary isn\'t rejection — it\'s a form of honesty that actually makes real connection possible.

Learning as a form of reverence

The wish to grow is your life force itself. It\'s your way of showing reverence for the gift that your life is: doing something with it, going deeper, becoming more.

You don\'t have to make yourself small to come close to the sacred. You may long greatly and stay soft.

To carry with you

  • I may long without judging myself.
  • Wanting enough is taking care of myself, not selfishness.
  • Money is energy, and it may flow to me.
  • I may ask for respect, and I carry my worth within me.
  • My boundaries protect my love, they don\'t block it.
  • I don\'t have to please everyone to be worthwhile.
